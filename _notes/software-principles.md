Principles are one layer removed from [[XP Values|values]]. They’re not as abstract as "we value respect" but they are still a loftier guidance for "how we see our work" rather than practices, which are closer to "how we do work" or "what we do day-to-day". Each of these principles connects back directly to one or more value that it supports.
