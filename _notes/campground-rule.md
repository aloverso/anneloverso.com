In software, the **campground rule** is the simply-put concept of *leave the codebase cleaner than you found it*.

If you see something that could be improved, you should take the opportunity (aka [opportunistic refactoring](https://martinfowler.com/bliki/OpportunisticRefactoring.html)) to fix it then and there.

Because we embrace the principle of [[shared context / shared responsibility|shared responsibility]], it doesn't matter if the code in question was written by you or someone else. A day ago or a year ago. If it could be improved, leave the campground cleaner than when you found it.

A team practicing the campground rule will [[continuous improvement|continuously improve]] their codebase and keep it clean and manageable when the time comes for larger changes or refactorings.
